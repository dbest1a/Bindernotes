## Style Prompt

BinderNotes tutorial videos use a premium dark education-SaaS look: layered charcoal panels, crisp white type, soft cyan/teal/violet accents, and clean product-motion language. The videos should feel like polished app walkthroughs for paying students: clear, calm, high-contrast, and fast enough to respect attention while leaving every label readable.

## Colors

- `#070b14` - deep app canvas
- `#111827` - primary product panels
- `#1e293b` - raised cards and module chrome
- `#32d5c8` - primary teal action/click accents
- `#7c5cff` - violet secondary accent
- `#dbeafe` - bright instructional text

## Typography

- Inter or system sans-serif for UI labels, captions, and callouts
- Use heavy weights only for scene titles and key click labels

## What NOT to Do

- No stock footage or generic classroom imagery
- No emoji decorations
- No low-contrast gray captions
- No busy backgrounds behind instructional text
- No fake pages that do not exist in BinderNotes
